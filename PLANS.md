# Execution Plans

An ExecPlan is a restartable record of the goal, current code, decisions, paths, exact commands and acceptance gates. Keep `Progress`, `Surprises & Discoveries`, `Decision Log` and `Outcomes & Retrospective` current. Distinguish planned services from implemented services and fake-provider tests from measured remote behavior. Each milestone must ship a testable behavior, a design/ADR update, a failure exercise and a learning explanation. Do not call the overall architecture implemented when only a milestone is complete.
